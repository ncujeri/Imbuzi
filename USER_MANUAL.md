# iMbuzi Smart — User Manual
### *Measure & Manage Livestock*

---

## What Is iMbuzi Smart?

iMbuzi Smart is a smartphone app designed for small to medium livestock farmers — especially goat farmers. It runs directly in your phone's browser, works **offline in the field** (no internet needed), and syncs your data to the cloud when you get back to Wi-Fi.

No paper records. No lost notebooks. Everything about your flock in one place.

---

## Getting Started

### Install on Your Phone

1. Open your phone browser and go to the app URL
2. Tap the browser menu → **"Add to Home Screen"** (iOS) or **"Install App"** (Android)
3. The app icon appears on your home screen — it opens like a normal app, no app store needed

---

## Features

### 1. Animals 🐐

**Add an animal:**
- Tap **Animals** → **+** button
- Fill in: Name, Tag number, Species, Breed, Sex, Date of Birth
- Set how you got it: **Born on Farm** (link to sire & dam) or **Purchased** (who you bought from and when)
- Add photos from your camera or gallery
- Save

**Find an animal:**
- Use the search bar (search by name or tag number)
- Filter by status: Active, Sold, Deceased, Market Ready, Quarantined

**Edit an animal:**
- Tap the pencil ✏ button on any animal card

---

### 2. Digital Passport 📋

Every animal has a passport — a single page that shows everything about it:

- Identity: name, tag, breed, sex, origin
- Full weight history with total gain
- All medication records with withdrawal period status
- Sale information (if sold)
- Extra photos

**Share or print:**
- Tap **↑ Share** to save/send as an image (for WhatsApp, email, etc.)
- Tap **PDF** to print or save as PDF

**Access:** Tap any animal → passport opens. Or go to **Passport** in the menu.

---

### 3. Weigh Animal ⚖️

Track weight over time to measure growth and feed efficiency.

1. Go to **Weigh Animal** (from Home dashboard or sidebar)
2. Select the animal
3. Enter the weight in kg
4. Choose method: Scale, Tape Measure, Estimate
5. Add notes if needed
6. Save

The passport shows all weight entries with a **total gain** calculation (e.g. "+18.4 kg over 92 days").

---

### 4. Medical Log 💉

Record every treatment, vaccination, and health event.

1. Go to **Medical Log** from the dashboard
2. Select the animal
3. Enter: Medication name, date, dosage, vet name
4. Set **withdrawal period** (days before slaughter is safe) — the app tracks this automatically
5. Save

**Withdrawal tracking:** The passport shows a red badge (ACTIVE) or green badge (CLEARED) next to each treatment. Never accidentally sell an animal in withdrawal again.

---

### 5. Record Mating 🤝

Track breeding events and predict kidding dates.

1. Go to **Record Mating** from the dashboard or sidebar
2. Select the buck (male) and doe (female)
3. Enter the mating date
4. The app automatically calculates the **expected kidding date** (150-day gestation)
5. Add notes (natural, pen mating, AI, etc.)
6. Save

**Kidding Watch alerts** appear on your home dashboard when a doe is within the alert window of her expected kidding date.

---

### 6. Log Heat 🔥

Track heat cycles to know when a doe is ready to mate.

1. Go to **Log Heat** from the dashboard
2. Select the doe
3. Enter the observation date
4. Set intensity: Mild / Strong / Standing heat
5. Describe the signs you observed
6. Save

The app shows **next expected heat** (+21 days) and keeps a history of each doe's cycle.

---

### 7. Cost & Expense Ledger 💰

Know exactly how much each animal is costing you.

1. Go to **Log Cost** from the dashboard
2. Select the animal (or leave blank for a farm-wide cost)
3. Choose a category: Feed, Medication, Vet, Transport, Labour, Other
4. Enter amount (in Rands), date, and description
5. Save

View all entries in the **Ledger** — sorted by date, filterable by animal or category.

---

### 8. Sale P&L Report 📈

When you sell an animal, see if you made a profit.

1. Edit the animal → change Status to **Sold**
2. Enter: Sale Price, Date Sold, Buyer Name
3. Save

Then tap the **📈 P&L** button on the animal card or passport. You'll see:

- Sale Price
- All direct costs broken down by category (Feed, Vet, Medication, etc.)
- Individual cost entries with dates
- **Net Profit or Loss** in large text (green = profit, red = loss)
- **ROI %** — return on your investment

---

### 9. Settings ⚙️

- Toggle **Inbreeding Check** — warns you if a buck and doe share parents
- Toggle **Heat Cycle Tracking**
- Set **Kidding Watch** days (how many days before expected kidding to start alerting)
- Enable/disable push notifications for kidding, weaning, heat cycles, and withdrawal expiry

---

## Working Offline

iMbuzi Smart works without internet. All data is saved to your phone.

When you're back online, the app automatically syncs any changes to the server. You'll see the sync status in the top bar:

- **Syncing...** (with count of pending items)
- **Synced** ✓
- **Offline** — data saved locally, will sync when connected
- **Failed** — tap Retry to try again

---

## Tips & Tricks

- **Search is instant** — start typing a name or tag number to find animals immediately
- **Passport sharing** — use the Share button to send a goat's full profile to a buyer on WhatsApp
- **Cost tracking before you sell** — log feed, vet, and transport costs as you go so your P&L is always ready
- **Photos matter** — add at least one photo per animal; it shows on the passport and makes identification easy
- **Batch mating** — record each buck-doe pairing separately so each doe gets her own kidding date prediction
